# Fames — Restaurant Website, Jalalpur Jattan

A modern, mobile-first 1-page restaurant website for **Fames**, a fast-food restaurant at Lakar Mandi, near Meezan Bank, Jalalpur Jattan, 50780, Pakistan.

**Live demo:** https://asjadfar.github.io/fames-restaurant-website/ (once published — see below)

## What it shows
- Sticky navigation with mobile menu + floating call button
- Hero with Fames' own cheese-pull pizza photo, 4.4★ Google rating
- Student + Shopkeeper 10% OFF (12–5 PM) and Free Delivery till 2 AM banner
- About — women-owned, dine-in & takeout, late-night food, free parking, wheelchair-accessible
- Full menu with category tabs + live search — 60+ items with Rs. prices:
  Starters, Fries & Sandwiches, Burgers, Rolls & Wraps, Cheese Sticks,
  Al Ikram Fames Special Injected Broast (Half 650 / Quarter 1,050 / Dosti 1,650 / Full 2,150 / Family 4,100),
  Traditional / Signature / Doner Pizzas (New Menu 2026 ranges), Train Pizza 3,250, Others
- Deals #1, #3, #4, #7, #8 + Murshad Deal (contents listed, call for price)
- Gallery — all 11 real Fames photos from Google Maps + menu flyers, no stock photos
- Reviews — 4.4 stars from 21 Google reviews, paraphrased customer quotes
- Visit Us — hours (Daily 12 PM–2 AM, dine-in till 10 PM, delivery till 2 AM), both phones
  +92 309 9979739 and 053-3592800, Instagram @fames.pk1, embedded Google Map
- SEO meta tags (title, description, Open Graph)

## Photos
All photos in `images/` are Fames' own photos downloaded from their Google Maps gallery

- Mobile-first, fully responsive
